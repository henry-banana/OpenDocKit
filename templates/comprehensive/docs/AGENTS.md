## Editing rules

Keep fact ownership clear. Update source and affected summaries/routes together. Active specs/designs/plans/tasks evolve with real scope/work; closed records retain history via corrections/successors. Role briefs need short factual summaries with links, not copied full source documents.

## Evidence and retention

Shared records can be proposed and tracked without claiming acceptance. Actual checks/approvals must have evidence. Keep private plans/tasks/sessions/handoffs and raw outputs in the selected personal paths under .agents or native/external storage; shared .agents/skills remains separately tracked.
