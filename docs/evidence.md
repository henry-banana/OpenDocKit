# Evidence and support claims

## Source-based proposals

[Source notes](sources.md) identify conceptual and format inputs to the library.
Historical research or a downloaded document is not evidence that every upstream
repository was fully reviewed. Cite primary sources for specific claims and
separate observed practice from a proposed improvement.

## Mechanical and real-world evidence

Validators check structure, paths, index coverage, local links and selected syntax.
A passing regression suite proves behavior against its test cases, not prose
quality or success in a copied application. Author review is not independent
review; native syntax checks are not client-loading trials.

No measured time/token gains, universal applicability, regulatory certification
or downstream product correctness are established by file counts or static PASS.
Historical example/evaluation assets are not present in this checkout and are not
reported as completed adoption or effectiveness trials.

## Evaluating usefulness

Before comparing layouts, define equal required facts and concrete reader tasks.
Measure correctness, source accuracy, acceptance/maintenance errors and resume
accuracy before considering speed or token counts. Record revision, environment,
commands, evaluator, failures/skips and actual evidence. Missing metrics remain
unknown; label proposed trials and checks not run explicitly.
