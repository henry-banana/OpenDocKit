# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenDocKit welcomes people with different backgrounds, identities, abilities and
levels of experience. We aim for a community where people can ask questions,
contribute and disagree without harassment or humiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people respectfully and assume questions deserve a useful answer.
- Critique proposals and work with concrete reasoning; keep criticism separate
  from judgments about the person.
- Be receptive to feedback, acknowledge mistakes and give people room to learn.
- Respect accessibility needs, communication differences and others' time.
- Protect personal information and obtain permission before sharing private
  conversations or identifying details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, personal attacks,
deliberate disclosure of private information and repeated disruptive conduct are
not acceptable. Retaliation against someone for reporting a concern is also
unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, contribution discussions
and other community spaces managed by OpenDocKit. It also applies when someone
represents the project in a public space.

## Reporting a concern

Contact the maintainers through an established project contact route. If no
private route is available, open a
[brief issue requesting one](https://github.com/henry-banana/OpenDocKit/issues/new)
without naming affected people or posting sensitive evidence. Share details only
after the route and its privacy limits are clear.

Include the relevant interaction, what happened and any immediate needs. Where
possible, a concern involving a maintainer should be reviewed by another
maintainer. Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Response and enforcement

Maintainers review context and relevant evidence, hear affected parties where
appropriate and handle reports with discretion. They may request a correction,
issue a warning, remove content or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation according to the severity and repetition of the behavior.

Maintainers should explain the decision and expected next steps to affected
participants where appropriate. A participant may request reconsideration through
the established contact route, including relevant context or new evidence.
Reporting channels and maintainer availability determine response times; no
fixed response deadline is promised.
